Frequently asked questions

Questions on your mind

Is the first consultation free or chargeable?

The first introductory session is completely free. We hear your issue and explain the way ahead. Fees for a detailed consultation are told upfront — no surprises.

Will my information remain confidential?

Yes. Everything you share stays within lawyer-client privilege and is never shared with any third party.

Is an online consultation as effective?

Absolutely. Most initial consultations and document reviews work well over a video call. For court appearances, we connect you with a local lawyer.

How long does document drafting take?

Standard documents (wills, agreements, contracts) are usually ready in 2–4 working days. Complex matters get a timeline upfront.

Do you take matters outside your city?

Yes, through our network of partner lawyers across India, we match you with the right lawyer for your city and court.
